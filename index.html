mission

Teaching kids to think like architects.

We don't teach prompts. We guide every student through the full lifecycle of a real product — the way professional creators work.

Empower students to think as architects of their own ideas — guiding them through the full development lifecycle, from initial spark to a live, AI-powered web platform.

STEP 01

⚙
STEP 02

Build the prototype

🚀
STEP 03

Ship a real platform

Deploy a live, AI-powered web product on Replit. Share it. Iterate. Be a founder.
